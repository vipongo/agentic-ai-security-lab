# Investment Policy

## Balanced Portfolios

Balanced investment portfolios should maintain diversified exposure
across asset classes.

A typical balanced allocation may contain approximately:

- 50% equities
- 35% bonds
- 10% alternative investments
- 5% cash

Actual allocations depend on the customer's investment objectives,
risk tolerance, liquidity requirements and investment horizon.

## Risk Assessment

A customer's risk profile must be assessed before making an
investment recommendation.

Available risk profiles include:

- Conservative
- Balanced
- Aggressive
